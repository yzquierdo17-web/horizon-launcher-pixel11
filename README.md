# Horizon Launcher

A clean, minimal home screen for the Google Pixel 11 Pro XL, built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

## Features
- Big clock and date (tap the clock for alarms, the date for your calendar)
- Dock of up to 5 apps with a frosted background
- Swipe up, or tap **Search apps**, to open the app drawer
- Instant search: type, then press Go to open the first match
- Long-press an app to add or remove it from the dock, see App info, or uninstall
- Long-press the home screen for wallpaper and default home app settings
- Colors follow your wallpaper (Material You)
- Pull down at the top of the drawer, or press Back, to close it

## Getting the app
Every push to `main` builds an APK automatically:
1. Open the **Actions** tab and tap the latest **Build Horizon APK** run.
2. Under **Artifacts**, download **Horizon-APK** and unzip it.
3. Open the `.apk` on your phone and allow the install.
4. Press Home and choose **Horizon**, or go to Settings → Apps → Default apps → Home app.
